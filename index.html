FAQ

Good to Know

How far ahead should I book?

Events need at least 7 days notice. Cookie preorders need 1 day.

What do you bring to my event?

Our cookies, fresh lemonade, dispensers, cups, napkins, and our own display table with decor for our station only. We do not provide tables, chairs or decor for the rest of your event.

Do you deliver?

Event delivery, setup and cleanup are free up to 25 miles from Austin 78747, then a flat $50 delivery fee. Cookie preorders can be picked up free or delivered for $10 (10 miles or under) or $20 (10 to 20 miles). We do not deliver preorders over 20 miles.

Can I order lemonade without an event?

Not right now. Our lemonade is made in a commercial kitchen and is available with booked event packages only.

Do you offer vegan or nut-free options?

Vegan cookies are available on request. Our home kitchen uses nuts, so we cannot offer nut-free cookies or guarantee any item is allergen-free.

What is the minimum cookie order?

One 5-pack. You can order as many packs as you like.

How do I pay?

We confirm your date, then send you an invoice. For events you can pay a 50% deposit to hold your date (balance invoiced before the event) or pay in full. Preorders are paid in full.
