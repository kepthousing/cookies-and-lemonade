A few helpful details

Before you order.

Here's a little guidance to help you plan. For anything special, use the order inquiry and we'll discuss the details with you.

How much notice should I give?

Please allow 2 business days for cookie orders and 1 week for events and larger orders.

How is lemonade sold?

Lemonade is sold by the gallon. Original is $12 per gallon; cucumber, strawberry, and peach are $14 per gallon.

What cookie boxes are available?

The Dozen Box is $40 for 12 cookies; the Gift Box is $45 for 12 cookies with ribbon and a card; the Party Box is $76 for 24 cookies; and the Crowd Box is $144 for 48 cookies.

Can I ask about catering or a custom order?

Yes. Use the inquiry form for large orders, catering, events, and special requests. Final details and availability will be confirmed separately.

What about allergens?

The listed cookies contain wheat, milk, eggs, and soy. The kitchen uses nuts, vegan options are available on request, and products may contain common allergens.
